# Ethics Guide

## Perfectly fine
- Using AI to research companies and roles
- Help with writing structure and grammar
- Customizing your *real* experiences for different positions
- Generating multiple versions of your resume format
- Practicing interview answers with AI feedback

## Not OK
- Inventing experiences or skills
- Claiming degrees or certifications you haven't earned
- Making up projects or achievements
- Fabricating employment dates or job titles
- Creating fake references or recommendations

## Gray areas, handle carefully
- *Skill enhancement*: say "developing" for skills you're learning; don't claim expertise
- *Project descriptions*: improve how you describe real projects, don't exaggerate scope or your role
- *Enthusiasm*: AI can express genuine interest more effectively, but the interest must be real

## Practical guidelines
Start with facts; enhance, don't invent; stay true to your voice; verify everything.

Good-practice prompt example: "Help me describe my capstone project more effectively. Here are the facts: [actual project details, technologies used, outcomes]. The job requires experience with data analysis and teamwork. Help me highlight relevant aspects without exaggerating my role."

## Red Flag Check
1. Could I confidently discuss every claim in an interview?
2. Are all the facts verifiable if an employer checks?
3. Does this sound like something I would actually say?
4. Am I comfortable with this representing me professionally?

## Privacy
Safe to share with AI: degree and graduation year, general project descriptions, public company information, job posting requirements, career interests and goals.
Keep private: specific salary numbers from current/past jobs, confidential project details, personal contact information, login credentials, references' personal details. Don't put SSN or full address in prompts.

## Respecting others' privacy
Don't scrape personal contact information from LinkedIn or company websites; use publicly available information only (company websites, job postings, press releases); respect communication preferences and don't spam with AI-generated messages; never use AI to impersonate others or create fake personas.

## Automation boundaries
AI prepares materials; **the human clicks submit**; no auto-submission.

## Long-term view
Reputation matters more than any single application; when in doubt, err on the side of transparency and honesty.
